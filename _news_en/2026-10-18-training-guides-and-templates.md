This update turns each stage into concrete work: what to check before starting, what to do, what to submit, how completion is assessed and how to respond when requirements have not yet been met.

## Choose your pathway
{: #pathways}

- [Undergraduate research training](/en/undergraduate-research.html): orientation, reading and small reproduction tasks, followed by research practice, reflection and archiving.
- [Undergraduate final-year project](/en/bachelor.html): topic selection, proposal, design and implementation, interim review, thesis, defense and final submission.
- [Academic competitions](/en/competition.html): check the current rules and eligibility, form a team, validate the work, submit and reflect.
- [Master's study](/en/master.html): topic selection, literature review, proposal review, interim review, thesis writing, research-output requirements, similarity and non-academic-error checks, external review, defense, degree procedures and archiving.

## Turn requirements into materials
{: #materials}

The [Template Library](/en/templates.html) contains 24 Markdown templates that you can download, preview or copy. Search by name or category, or download the complete set. Use the university's applicable official forms when required; these general templates support drafting and self-checking.

[My Stage Checklist](/en/planner.html) generates a checklist for your pathway and stage, saves it in the current browser and lets you export it. A checked box records your own assessment; it does not replace supervisor review or formal university approval.

## Confirm formal requirements before starting
{: #requirements}

[Policies and Local Requirements](/en/policies.html) distinguishes national baseline rules, university requirements and practical suggestions. Confirm program duration, output eligibility, checking standards, and review and defense dates for your cohort, discipline and degree type. Shared guides also cover [day-to-day work](/en/management.html), [research integrity](/en/integrity.html) and [data and material archiving](/en/archive.html).
