The site now has **News** and **Stories** sections to help members follow upcoming work and share what students learn through research and practice.

## News: follow arrangements and requirements
{: #news}

Group arrangements, milestone requirements, event notices and related updates will appear by date in [News](/en/news.html). The home page also shows the three most recent posts.

Notices with deadlines will identify the intended readers, the task, the schedule and how to complete it. For formal university procedures, follow the applicable official notice.

## Stories: learn from students' experiences
{: #stories}

[Stories](/en/students.html) will introduce students' learning journeys, representative work and reflections. Accounts will focus on concrete experiences in study, research and practice, and will be published after the material has been confirmed.

## Other sections
{: #other-sections}

- [Resources](/en/resources.html): training guides, research methods, development tools, writing materials and archiving guidance.
- [Outcomes](/en/outcomes.html): verified work in academic research, engineering, competitions and student development.
- [About](/en/about.html): the group's approach to learning, member roles, ways of working and public contact channels.

The undergraduate, master's and doctoral guides remain available for students to consult at their current stage.
