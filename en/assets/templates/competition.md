# Competition planning, submission and reflection

Purpose: manage rules, team responsibilities, development, validation and submissions for undergraduate or master's participation in disciplinary, entrepreneurship, innovation or engineering-design competitions. Coordinate participation with courses, research and graduation milestones. Registration or a demonstration is not completion of the competition.

Instructions: verify the official website and rules for the current edition before committing resources. Record actual registration, university selection, final-round and submission milestones. Guidance, university recommendation and an award are different states. When using team outputs or existing research, agree credit, permissions and disclosure scope first.
