# How to use the training templates

These 28 downloadable Markdown templates can be copied and completed for undergraduate research training, graduation projects and competitions, and for master's and doctoral research, professional practice, theses and archiving. They organize work and evidence; current official documents and forms issued by your university or school take precedence. Applicable templates include dedicated fields for doctoral qualification, the research agenda and integration of contributions.

## Getting started

1. Choose a template for your current stage, keep the original as a blank master, and complete a copy in your project.
2. Suggested filename: `YYYY-MM-DD-project-code-document-name-v01.md`. Link code, reports, run records and official receipts using paths relative to the project.
3. Replace `[Complete]` with actual information. For unknown items, record “To be verified, owner, verification date.” Explain why an item is not applicable. Mark work that has not been performed as “Not performed.”
4. After checking your work, ask the person responsible to review it. Record feedback, corrections and the confirmation date. Do not prefill “Passed” before review. University approvals must still follow the formal process.

## Boundaries to preserve

- Record formal requirements, guidance and personal plans separately. Do not invent universal publication counts, similarity thresholds, practice durations or attendance hours.
- Label live runs, saved-record replay, authors' reported results, constructed examples and unverified hypotheses separately. The existence of files or a successful script check does not replace research validation.
- “Non-academic-error checks” refers here to checks for presentation and other non-academic errors. Verify the official name and checklist with your school; handle these separately from similarity checking and AI-use declarations.
- Submission, acceptance and publication; registration, submission and winning an award; and application and grant of an intellectual-property right are different states. Record the actual supporting documents.
- Do not put account credentials, unnecessary personal information or private absolute paths in templates. For restricted data, record only authorized-use information, version identifiers and a controlled location.
- Manage anonymous examination, defense and final archive versions separately. Archives should include an actually generated file inventory, checksums, a recovery entry point and a record of unfinished items.
