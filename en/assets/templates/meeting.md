# Meeting, discussion and decision record

Purpose: record facts, decisions and actions from group meetings, individual supervision or collaborative discussions, reducing conflicting interpretations afterward. This record coordinates tasks; it does not replace signatures or formal-system approval required for program procedures.

Instructions: add agenda items and materials before the meeting, record decisions and reasons during it, and confirm owners and deadlines afterward. Distinguish suggestions, agreed decisions and pending approvals. Unconfirmed judgments must not become commitments attributed to a supervisor or team. Follow actual attendance and leave rules; do not invent a universal requirement for time online.
