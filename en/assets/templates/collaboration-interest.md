# Research and competition collaboration interest

Purpose: help students at this institution, other institutions and overseas introduce their interests and availability, and start a conversation about research or competition collaboration. Undergraduate, master's and doctoral students can use this form, including students new to research.

Write briefly in Chinese or English, usually within 1–2 pages. Use “to discuss” for unknown items and “none yet” where appropriate. This form sets no universal GPA, publication-count or university-ranking threshold. Identity documents, transcripts, date of birth and nationality are not needed for this first contact.

## 2. What you want to explore and learn

- Specific question or area of interest: [Use 2–3 sentences in your own words; start from reading, coursework or a problem you have encountered].
- Capabilities you hope to develop: [For example, explaining a paper, completing a small reproduction, designing experiments, building a prototype or coordinating a competition team].

- Representative work and your contribution: [Share 1–2 course projects, reading notes, small exercises, other work or studies; provide a link or short description, explain your contribution and identify help from collaborators or tools].

If you do not yet have a finished piece of work, describing a learning attempt or a small exercise you plan to try is also welcome. Share only material you have permission to provide.

## 5. A first-contact message you can adapt

Use a public contact channel listed on the [supervisor's public website](https://h-jp.github.io/). Suggested email subject: **Research / competition collaboration enquiry | [study level] | [interest keyword]**.

> Hello, my name is [preferred name], and I am a [study level] student in [subject] at [institution]. I am interested in [specific question or area] and would like to develop [capability] through [research / competition] collaboration. I have tried [course project, reading, exercise or research; briefly state your contribution], with shareable material at [link, or explain if none is available]. I hope to participate [remotely / another way] from [start] to [end], for approximately [time] per week. My time zone is [zone], and I can communicate in [Chinese / English]. I would appreciate discussing whether there is a suitable small task or collaboration opportunity, particularly with guidance on [specific need]. I have included a brief expression of interest and look forward to a conversation. Thank you.

Before sending:

- [ ] Interests and availability are specific.
- [ ] Links work and I am allowed to share the material.
- [ ] My contribution and the support I hope for are clear.

A short email body can serve as the expression of interest; a duplicate attachment is unnecessary.

## 6. After the first conversation

If both parties wish to continue, use a [research task card](research-task.md) to agree a small task suited to your current background, and a [learning and growth review](learning-review.md) to record attempts, feedback and next steps. If formal hosting, sending or joint training is involved, use the [cross-institution training and supervision draft](joint-training-plan.md) to connect the necessary arrangements.

After a reply or an agreed task, proceed according to the discussion; formal hosting, registration or credit matters still depend on their respective confirmations. Send the completed expression of interest to the prospective supervisor; there is no need to publish it on the website.
