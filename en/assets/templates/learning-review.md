# Learning and growth review

> For undergraduate research, capstone projects, competitions, master's study and doctoral research. Use one record to connect capability goals, study and practice, actual evidence, feedback and next steps. You may reference this template from an existing weekly or stage report to avoid duplicate paperwork. It supports learning and supervision and does not constitute university approval or a formal assessment outcome.

## 7. Check before sharing

- [ ] The capability goal can be observed through explanation, action, comparison or judgment.
- [ ] Starting points, practice and review refer to actual records with dates or versions.
- [ ] Reading materials have identifiable sources and are relevant to the task.
- [ ] Observations, interpretations, assumptions and plans are distinguished; unfinished work retains an accurate status.
- [ ] Output quality, individual contributions and capability growth are described separately.
- [ ] Assistance received and your checking process are clear; support needs are recorded.
- [ ] Feedback has a response, reasons, evidence of changes and recheck arrangements.
- [ ] Transfer and next steps are actionable; formal training conditions have been checked separately.
- [ ] Shared materials exclude unrelated personal information, secrets and research material not approved for disclosure.

## Example entry: a plan awaiting execution

This is an illustration, not a report of any student's completed work.

- Capability: explain the difference between a test failure and a code defect.
- Starting evidence: currently only a paraphrase of an error message; environmental factors have not been checked.
- Learning task: read the project's testing documentation and design checks that distinguish environmental factors from code behaviour.
- Planned evidence: commands, environment details, outputs and an interpretation table; enter actual results after execution.
- Support needed: ask a supervisor whether the two proposed explanations have appropriate distinguishing checks.
- Transfer task: use a different failure record and explain its possible causes and remaining evidence gaps.
