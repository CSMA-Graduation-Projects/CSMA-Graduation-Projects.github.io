# Thesis writing and version checks

Purpose: organize writing, review and version control for an undergraduate thesis, master's thesis or doctoral dissertation. University templates and school notices govern format, chapter requirements and submission procedures. This record links each claim to its evidence.

Instructions: establish chapter arguments and evidence before drafting prose. Check content, citations, layout and file status separately before formal submission. Preserve anonymous examination, defense and final archive versions separately; renaming a file does not make those versions interchangeable.
