# Topic justification and scope

Purpose: compare and select topics for undergraduate graduation projects, research training or master's research. Establish whether the problem is real, where existing approaches fall short and whether available resources are sufficient, rather than starting with a title full of technical terms. The final topic must follow the school's confirmation procedure.

Instructions: record evidence before making judgments. “Expected to work” is a hypothesis, not a result. For each candidate topic, specify at least one condition that could lead to abandoning it. Cite the formal requirements for the relevant program when discussing originality and output types; do not invent a universal standard.
