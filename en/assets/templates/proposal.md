# Research proposal and review preparation

Purpose: organize the proposed work, preliminary review and revision tracking before an undergraduate graduation-project, master's or doctoral proposal review. Use the school's official form for submission. This template is a working justification or evidence attachment and does not replace university approval.

Instructions: connect problem, evidence, method, validation and schedule. Label anticipated results as expectations; preliminary experiments must describe only work actually completed. Follow the current school notice for length, eligibility, timing and number of copies.
