Undergraduate research training

Research training prioritizes skill development and verifiable evidence. It does not guarantee publications, awards or admission to further study. Before reusing results in a capstone or thesis, check institutional requirements, your contribution and the permitted scope of reuse.

  1. 01Orientation and mutual fitBefore formally accepting a task
  2. 02Tools, literature and research practiceEarly in the trial period, adapted to your starting skills
  3. 03Reading, replication and small tasksBefore formal project planning
  4. 04Project plan and task breakdownAfter the small task is accepted, before sustained work
  5. 05Execution, weekly reports and reviewsThroughout the project, at the agreed frequency
  6. 06Result verification and communicationBefore closure, publication submission, competition entry or public release
  7. 07Closure, archiving and next stepsBefore project completion, transition to a capstone or withdrawal

Undergraduate capstone / bachelor's thesis

The capstone must demonstrate your own integrated learning. Before reusing research, competition or internship work, check topic suitability, individual contribution, permissions and institutional rules. All required capstone stages still apply.

  1. 01Requirements, topic and task briefDuring the department's topic-selection and task-allocation window
  2. 02Literature review and proposalBefore the formal proposal review, allowing time for revision
  3. 03Requirements analysis and detailed designBefore substantial implementation
  4. 04Implementation and mid-program reviewFrom design confirmation to the department's mid-program review window
  5. 05Testing, experiments and analysisAfter core implementation, before finalizing conclusions
  6. 06Thesis writing and compliance checksWrite throughout the project; prepare a complete draft before checks
  7. 07Defense preparation and participationAfter eligibility is confirmed, following the defense notice
  8. 08Final revisions, archiving and handoverAfter the defense, before the department's archiving deadline

Competitions and innovation practice

Eligibility, format, fees and award recognition may change between editions. An award does not automatically replace course or degree requirements. Check rules, permissions and contributions before reusing a project.

  1. 01Competition, eligibility and rulesBefore registration or equipment purchases
  2. 02Team, topic and ownership agreementBefore team registration and development
  3. 03Prototype, budget and registrationCheck the official registration window separately; validate high-risk assumptions before committing major resources.
  4. 04Implementation, replication and internal acceptanceBefore formal submission, allowing time for fixes
  5. 05Submission, competition and confirmed resultsFollow the official deadline, time zone and session
  6. 06Reflection, expense settlement and archivingAfter the competition ends or withdrawal is agreed

Master's research and degree pathway

Academic master's programs emphasize academic research capability. Professional master's programs also address professional practice and application. Specific outputs and standards depend on the discipline or professional degree category. This page does not set institutional output counts, publication levels, screening percentages or deadlines.

  1. 01Enrollment, study plan and working agreementsComplete after enrollment as required by the study plan; review each semester
  2. 02Exploration and topic selectionBefore proposal review, after necessary preliminary tests
  3. 03Literature review and research positioningDraft before proposal review; update throughout the research
  4. 04Research plan and formal proposal reviewWithin the institution's prescribed proposal-review window
  5. 05Research, professional practice and recordsContinue after proposal review, with reflection at agreed intervals
  6. 06Mid-program review and annual assessmentFollow the applicable rules for each; do not assume one replaces the other
  7. 07Thesis structure, draft and revisionWrite throughout the research; complete substantive review before external submission
  8. 08Research outputs and degree eligibilityUnderstand at enrollment; confirm each item before the degree application
  9. 09Formatting, similarity, error and AI-use checksFollow the institution's screening and thesis-review submission windows
  10. 10Anonymous thesis review and responsesWithin the institution's designated review window
  11. 11Defense application and formal defenseAfter formal defense eligibility, following departmental notices
  12. 12Final revisions and degree proceduresAfter the defense, before the deadline for the current application round
  13. 13Research archive, handover and departureMaintain throughout the research; complete acceptance before departure

Doctoral Research and Degree Pathway

This is a framework for research and development, not a substitute for institutional procedures. Verify whether a qualifying assessment is required, its order relative to the proposal, annual and interim assessments, the timing of any preliminary defence, recognised outputs and degree application conditions. Annual reflection continues throughout the programme. Academic and professional doctorates may recognise different contributions; this page does not prescribe a common programme length, publication count, publication category or screening threshold.

  1. 01Programme Requirements, Supervision and Development PlanEstablish on enrolment and when supervision is arranged; review each semester and whenever requirements change
  2. 02Foundations, Research Methods and a Map of QuestionsBuild an initial foundation early in the programme and extend it as the research questions develop
  3. 03Qualifying Assessment and Research Readiness, Where ApplicableAt the formal qualifying assessment window; where no such assessment exists, use a research readiness discussion only
  4. 04Research Direction, Linked Studies and Formal ProposalWithin the institutional proposal window, once the necessary preliminary evidence is available
  5. 05Independent Research, Evidence Testing and IterationRepeat throughout the research period after the proposal; necessary exploratory work may begin earlier
  6. 06Ongoing Annual Reflection, Interim Assessment and ReplanningMaintain from enrolment and submit at institutional annual or interim milestones; research need not be complete
  7. 07Original Contributions, Output Recognition and Scholarly ExchangeContinue as individual studies yield verifiable findings; check again before the degree application
  8. 08Dissertation Synthesis and Academic Integrity ChecksWrite throughout the research and produce a complete reviewable version before formal submission
  9. 09Preliminary Defence, Dissertation Review and ResponsesFollow the institutional sequence and submission windows; verify separately whether and when a preliminary defence applies
  10. 10Formal Defence, Revision Approval and Degree ApplicationAfter formal defence eligibility is confirmed; complete revisions and degree procedures by the applicable deadlines
  11. 11Research Archive, Handover and Future DevelopmentArchive throughout the research; complete formal handover at a stage ending, graduation or departure

Plan backwards from official milestones

Enter official departmental deadlines first, then allow agreed time for supervisory review, revisions and system submission. Confirm lead times with the reviewer based on the workload; illustrative durations are not universal requirements. If delayed, record the impact, request support and complete any required change procedures.

Recording stage status

Not started → In progress → Awaiting review → Complete. Record blockers separately with causes, owners and the next review date. A student's report, material submission, supervisory review and formal institutional approval are distinct states.
