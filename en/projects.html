Projects and Topics

Required public fields

Suggested minimum public information

  • A stable ID and public title
  • Study pathway and public research area
  • A short summary cleared for release
  • Verifiable status and its source
  • Public links, where available
  • Date last verified
  • visibility / consentToPublish

Never publish

Information the project page must not contain

  • Grades, rankings, private evaluations or records of personal difficulties
  • Unpublished applications, papers, experiment data or patent content
  • Private repository, server or restricted-data addresses
  • Account credentials, tokens, passwords, keys or recovery codes
  • Names, photographs or achievements without the individual's permission

Publication workflow

From an internal project to a public entry

  1. Verify the facts: Confirm the title, status, participants, outputs and links.
  2. Verify the scope: Check for unpublished research, collaboration restrictions and intellectual property awaiting protection.
  3. Check privacy: Minimise disclosed data and exclude individual assessments and personal contact details.
  4. Confirm permission: Retain a traceable publication-permission record that supports withdrawal.
  5. Publish and review: Render only fields explicitly cleared for release, and periodically recheck links and status.
