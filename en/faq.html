Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about study pathways, milestone materials, formal requirements and using this site.

Which study pathway should I start with?

For an undergraduate graduation project or capstone, start with the graduation-project pathway. Undergraduates interested in research can use the research pathway. For a competition, use the competition pathway. Master's students should start with the master's pathway, and doctoral students with the doctoral guidance.

How can I identify my current stage?

Start with the milestone overview, then compare your position with each stage's entry conditions, activities, deliverables and completion criteria. Use the stage checklist to organise tasks. Ticking an item does not mean your supervisor or university has approved it.

Are dates on this site official department deadlines?

The suggested timing helps organise work. Official deadlines come from the university, department and cohort notices applicable to you. Complete the local requirements check before starting and update it when a new notice appears.

Where can I download templates?

The template library contains 24 editable Markdown templates. Preview, copy or download an individual template, or download the complete set. Fill in copies within your own approved access-controlled workspace. Use the applicable official university form when one is required.

Can I put progress reports, proposals, midterm materials or thesis drafts in a public repository?

Not by default. They may contain personal progress information, supervisor feedback, unpublished research, data or review information and should be kept in university systems or approved private storage. Publish only a version explicitly cleared for public release.

How should accounts and server details be handled during handover?

Use the platform's official process to transfer ownership, administrative roles and access to organisation resources. Rotate or revoke service keys as required. Do not hand over personal passwords or record credentials in ordinary documents or public pages. See the archiving and handover guide.

Why are there no examples on the project and achievements pages?

The repository does not yet contain verifiable records or permission to publish them. This site does not present examples as real projects, papers, patents, competition results or student achievements.

Does the program guarantee a publication, patent or award?

No. These are possible outcomes, dependent on the maturity of the work, applicable rules, intellectual property and external review. Developing capabilities and conducting the work responsibly also matter.

Can a login on this site protect private materials?

No. GitHub Pages serves a public static website. A front-end login cannot make HTML, JSON or other files in its public repository private. Sensitive information must be stored outside the public repository.

How can I contact the group or apply to join?

The GitHub organisation page is currently the only public entry point that can be verified from this repository. The responsible staff have yet to add an official email address, office location, recruitment dates or application channel. This site does not guess those details.
