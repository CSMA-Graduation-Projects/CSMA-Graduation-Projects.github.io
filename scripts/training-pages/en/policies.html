Identify the documents that apply before turning requirements into personal deadlines and a submission checklist. Distinguish group recommendations, examples from other universities and national rules.

Verify applicable requirements each academic year and graduation round

International students should also confirm teaching, thesis, abstract and defense languages, courses, language proficiency, project eligibility and administrative requirements. Use the international student checklist and its official sources. Choosing English on this website does not determine the rules that apply to your programme.

Begin the requirements record by identifying your university and department, discipline, entry cohort, degree type, mode of study, planned graduation round and program-plan version. For each rule, record the document name, issuing body, original link or official system location, publication and effective dates, scope, specific requirement, check date and checker. Mark an uncertain item “To be verified”; do not substitute a similar discipline's rules or a previous cohort's standards.

An uncertain rule need not prevent reading, research or preparation, but verify it before making the formal submission it governs. Contact the responsible academic or graduate-program staff early, record their response and obtain the formal basis so there is still time to act.

Turn formal requirements into an actionable plan

Work backwards from the university's final deadline to schedule supervisor review, revisions, checks, external review and confirmation of materials, leaving time for rework. Suggested intervals on this site are working arrangements; they do not replace mandatory minimum intervals, fixed rounds or deadlines. Academic and professional degrees, different cohorts and different study modes may follow different rules. A similar document title is not enough to establish applicability.

When a new notice appears, check whether it replaces an earlier document or applies only to a particular round, then update affected tasks. Ask the issuing body to resolve conflicting sources. Preserve old versions and record what replaced them. The university examples below illustrate real procedures; they do not identify this portal's institution or transfer another university's thresholds to its students.

China's national laws, education policies and research rules

01 · Academic Degrees Law

Adopted on 26 April 2024 and effective from 1 January 2025. It addresses degree-award conditions, procedures and quality assurance; check the awarding institution's rules for specific requirements. Distinguish a defense outcome, a degree application and the decision to award a degree, and identify the academic review channels.

Ministry of Education: Academic Degrees Law of the People's Republic of China

02 · Undergraduate thesis quality

Published on 7 January 2021 and effective from 1 January 2021. It covers topic significance, organisation of writing, logical construction, disciplinary competence and academic integrity. Students should maintain complete records throughout the work and final submission. Sampling-based thesis inspection cannot be reduced to a similarity score.

Ministry of Education: Interim Measures for Sampling Inspection of Undergraduate Theses and Graduation Projects

03 · Undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training

Published on 24 July 2019. It applies to the national undergraduate innovation and entrepreneurship training program and emphasises student ownership and learning through the process. Group research training may draw on its project initiation, progress management and completion methods. A project without formal approval cannot be described as part of the national program.

Ministry of Education: Administrative Measures for the National Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program

04 · Annual innovation competition rules

Published on 31 July 2026 and applicable only to the relevant tracks of the 2026 competition. Use it to examine eligibility, teams, tracks, materials and scheduling; registration for that edition has closed. Read the new official notice when preparing for a later edition rather than treating historical dates as current registration arrangements.

Ministry of Education: Notice on the China International College Students' Innovation Competition 2026

05 · Academic integrity in higher education

Ministry of Education Order No. 40, promulgated on 16 June 2016 and effective from 1 September 2016. It covers prevention, investigation, determination, handling and remedies. Authenticity, citation, authorship and research records require attention throughout the work; one detection result cannot replace a full assessment.

Measures for the Prevention and Handling of Academic Misconduct in Higher Education Institutions

06 · Quality management throughout graduate education

Published on 28 September 2020. It emphasises key program milestones and requirements appropriate to different degree categories. This site therefore presents proposal review, midterm review, external review, defense and correction separately. Their formal organisation and passing conditions depend on institutional rules.

Opinions on Further Strengthening Quality Management in Degree and Graduate Education

07 · Academic and professional graduate degrees

Published on 18 December 2023. Academic degrees emphasise knowledge innovation, while professional degrees emphasise innovation in practice; both are equally important. Whether practice-based outputs can support a degree application, and in what form, must be confirmed under the applicable rules.

Ministry of Education: Opinions on Advancing Differentiated Development of Academic and Professional Graduate Education

08 · Supervisors' responsibilities

Published on 11 November 2020. It requires supervisors to provide guidance, safeguard quality and protect students' rights. Day-to-day arrangements should clarify both parties' responsibilities and feedback mechanisms. Website recommendations do not authorise additional unconfirmed penalties or graduation barriers.

Ministry of Education: Code of Conduct for Graduate Supervisors

09 · Science and technology ethics review

Published on 8 October 2023 and marked valid on the official page at the source-check date. For activities within its scope involving research participants, personal information and related matters, determine review requirements and complete the process before the activity begins. Retrospective self-checking cannot replace approval.

Ministry of Science and Technology and other bodies: Interim Measures for Science and Technology Ethics Review

10 · Scientific data management

Dated 17 March 2018 and republished by the National Energy Administration on 27 April 2018. It principally covers scientific data activities supported by government budget funding and other situations specified in the measures. It supports provenance, quality, preservation and controlled sharing; it does not require all research data to be public.

General Office of the State Council: Measures for the Management of Scientific Data

Shanghai Jiao Tong University; published on 21 November 2025, effective from 1 September 2025, and applicable only to that university.
