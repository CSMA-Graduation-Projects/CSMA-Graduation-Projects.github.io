# Progress report and next-stage plan

Purpose: provide periodic reports for undergraduate research training, graduation projects and master's research, making actual progress, evidence, blockers and decisions easy to review. Use the agreed reporting period. Word counts and time online do not replace work quality.

Instructions: summarize work actually completed during the period, then compare it with previous commitments. Give a locatable artifact for every claim of completion. Mark work in progress, unperformed work and unverified claims separately. Plans, an open terminal or illustrative screenshots do not constitute completion evidence.
