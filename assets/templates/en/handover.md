# Stage handover and group departure

Purpose: transfer research material, systems and open tasks when personnel change, a student graduates, equipment is returned or collaboration ends. The incoming owner should understand the current state and how to continue. University rules still govern departure procedures and asset disposal.

Instructions: transfer only what is necessary; do not hand over personal accounts, passwords or unrelated personal material. The deliverer prepares an inventory and instructions; the recipient confirms after actual spot checks. Sending an archive file is not a completed handover.
