# Academic integrity, non-academic-error and similarity checks

Purpose: check academic integrity, non-academic errors, textual similarity and AI-use disclosure separately before proposal, midterm, examination, defense or archiving. These checks address different questions; one report cannot establish overall thesis compliance.

Instructions: “Non-academic-error checks” means checks for presentation and other non-academic errors in this guide. Verify the applicable name, checklist and process with your school. Similarity checking usually concerns textual similarity; AI-assistance declarations or related checks follow separate university rules. Record all three separately. This template sets no universal threshold: use the documents that apply to you.
