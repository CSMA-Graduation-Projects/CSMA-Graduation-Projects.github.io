# Undergraduate graduation-project task brief: working draft

Purpose: help the student and supervisor clarify the topic, tasks, technical requirements, schedule and submissions before the formal task brief is issued. This working draft does not replace the official university or school form, signatures, review or system issuance.

Instructions: align tasks with program learning objectives and feasible work. Do not simply assign an entire master's thesis or team project to one undergraduate. Research, design and engineering projects may produce different outputs, but all require verifiable individual work and assessment criteria.
