# Cross-institution training and supervision agreement: working draft

Purpose: agree objectives, responsibilities, resources and programme arrangements before hosting a student from another institution, sending a student to a partner institution or starting remote co-supervision. For undergraduate research, competitions, capstone projects, master's and doctoral study, including international students.

This is a working draft. It does not replace an institutional agreement, university approval, registration, formal assessment or degree recognition. Co-supervision does not automatically establish eligibility for a joint or dual degree. Keep unconfirmed items marked “pending confirmation”, with a contact and follow-up date; do not pre-enter approval.

Store completed records in a space accessible only to authorised participants. The public website provides blank templates only. Do not attach personal contact details, signatures, identity documents, unpublished research data or full agreements to a public introduction.

Sequence: [Read the joint training guide](https://csma-graduation-projects.github.io/en/joint-training.html) → complete this draft together → check formal requirements in the [milestone and recognition register](joint-training-recognition.md) → complete necessary procedures → work within the confirmed scope.
