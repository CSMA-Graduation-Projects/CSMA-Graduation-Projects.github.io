# Thesis examination preparation and point-by-point responses

Purpose: organize pre-examination checks, responses to examiners and version verification. University rules govern eligibility, anonymity, interpretation of outcomes and re-examination. This record does not replace a formal application or re-examination decision.

Instructions: first freeze the actual submission version and materials, then respond to each original comment. Do not alter or omit the substance of comments. Explain reasoned disagreements with evidence; never invent supplementary experiments. Manage anonymous and identified final versions separately and check anonymity against the specific procedure.
