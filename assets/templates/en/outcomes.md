# Output requirements and achievement register

Purpose: separately check formal degree-application or graduation output requirements and the papers, software, patents, competition or practice outputs actually produced. Academic master's, professional master's and undergraduate requirements may differ. Do not assume a universal publication count or equivalence between output types.

Instructions: first identify the applicable entry cohort, program category and current valid documents, then cite provisions individually. Ask the school about uncertain rules. Record research objectives, supervisory advice and formal graduation requirements separately; they do not replace one another. Submission, acceptance and publication are distinct states.
