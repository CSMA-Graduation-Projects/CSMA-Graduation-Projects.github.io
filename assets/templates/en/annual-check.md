# Institutional requirements and annual progress record

Purpose: check undergraduate, master's or doctoral progress at the end of a year, semester or stage and plan the next period. Cover courses, research, professional practice, communication, resources and records rather than focusing only on publication counts. Use the university's or school's official form for formal annual assessment.

Instructions: identify requirements applicable to your program category and entry cohort, recording actual status and evidence. Day-to-day management should follow agreed tasks, timely communication and necessary institutional rules. Do not invent universal office, online or attendance hours. Use the actual formal channels for leave and approval.
