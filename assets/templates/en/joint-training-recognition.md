# Cross-institution milestone and recognition register

Purpose: align both institutions' requirements with one training record, stating what to do, who organises each stage, where to submit, when it is due and who formally recognises it. Use alongside the [training and supervision working draft](joint-training-plan.md).

Principle: completed research, supervisory confirmation, institutional approval and formal recognition are four distinct states. Record each separately. A host institution's evaluation cannot automatically replace another body's formal recognition. Official notices and forms take precedence; do not assume universal credits, practice durations, publication counts or screening thresholds.

Store completed forms, comments and supporting documents in a space accessible to authorised parties. Publish blank templates only. Evidence links should point to actual records with appropriate access; do not upload unrelated personal information.
