# Defense preparation, questions and revision acceptance

Purpose: organize preparation, questions raised and subsequent revisions for an undergraduate graduation-project or master's thesis defense. The school's notice governs eligibility, panel composition, duration and procedure. A completed demonstration or pre-defense does not mean the formal defense has been passed.

Instructions: organize the presentation around question, method, evidence, contribution and limitations. Slides must agree with the submitted thesis; do not add unsupported claims at the last moment. The official university record determines the outcome. This form checks readiness and tracks revisions.
