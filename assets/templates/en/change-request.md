# Topic, plan and resource change record

Purpose: record evidence, impacts and a revised executable plan when research scope, timing, methods, data, responsibilities or resources change significantly. Changes to topics, supervisors, enrollment status or graduation milestones that require university approval must follow the separate formal procedure.

Instructions: preserve the original plan first, then explain why it should change, the alternatives and affected deliverables. A requested change is not an approved change. Distinguish draft, discussed, confirmed and formally approved states.
